Recipe 1: Pan-Fried Tofu with Garlic and Smoked Paprika

Ready in 20 minutes • 1 pan • No oven

Ingredients

  • 350g extra firm tofu
  • 15ml Bragg Liquid Aminos
  • 3g garlic powder
  • 3g smoked paprika
  • 1g black pepper
  • 1g fine sea salt
  • 15ml light olive oil

Instructions

  1. Press tofu 30 minutes. Pat completely dry with paper towels — dry tofu browns beautifully.
  2. Mix Bragg, garlic powder, paprika, pepper, and salt in a small bowl. Brush onto both sides of each tofu slice.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan on medium-high. Add tofu in a single layer.
  4. Cook 3 to 4 minutes without moving — let a golden crust form. Flip. Cook 2 to 3 more minutes.
  5. Serve over rice or in a bowl with Ginger-Miso Citrus Sauce or Sesame-Ginger Dressing from the Sauce section.
Nutrition per serving: Calories: ~180   Potassium: ~160mg   Sodium: ~290mg   Protein: ~18g

Servings: 1

Recipe 2: Egg Foo Young with Brown Gravy

Ready in 25 minutes • 1 pan • Comfort food any time of day

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 120g mung bean sprouts
  • 80g frozen peas and carrots mix
  • 40g green onions
  • 6g garlic
  • 2g fine sea salt
  • 1g black pepper
  • 15ml light olive oil
  • 240ml Pacific Foods Low Sodium Vegetable Broth
  • 20ml Bragg Liquid Aminos
  • 8g cornstarch
  • 5g fresh ginger
  • 2g garlic powder
  • 3ml sesame oil
  • 200g dry jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Start jasmine rice in rice cooker. While rice cooks make the egg foo young.
  2. In a large bowl beat all 6 eggs. Add sprouts, peas and carrots, green onions, garlic, salt, and pepper. Stir well.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large pan on medium. Pour in about 1/4 of the egg mixture to form one pancake about 12cm wide. Cook undisturbed 2 to 3 minutes until edges look set and bottom is golden. Flip and cook 1 to 2 more minutes. Repeat to make 4 pancakes total.
  4. MAKE THE GRAVY in the same pan: In a small cup mix cornstarch with 30ml of the cold broth until smooth with no lumps. Add remaining broth, Bragg, ginger, and garlic powder to the pan on medium heat. Bring to a gentle simmer. Pour in cornstarch mixture while stirring constantly. Gravy thickens within 60 seconds. Remove from heat. Stir in sesame oil.
  5. Serve 2 pancakes over rice per person. Pour gravy generously over the pancakes.
Nutrition per serving: Calories: ~480   Potassium: ~430mg   Sodium: ~420mg   Protein: ~24g

Servings: 1

Recipe 3: Tofu Scramble with Spinach and Cherry Tomato

Ready in 15 minutes • 1 pan • Great for any meal

Ingredients

  • 300g extra firm tofu
  • 80g fresh baby spinach
  • 80g cherry tomatoes
  • 60g yellow onion
  • 8g garlic
  • 2g turmeric
  • 2g garlic powder
  • 2g smoked paprika
  • 2g cumin
  • 1g fine sea salt
  • 1g black pepper
  • 15ml light olive oil
  • 15ml fresh lemon juice to finish

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan on medium. Add onion and cook 4 minutes until soft.
  2. Add garlic and stir 30 seconds. Add turmeric, garlic powder, paprika, and cumin. Stir 30 seconds.
  3. Crumble tofu into the pan. Add salt and pepper. Stir to coat in the spices.
  4. Let tofu sit undisturbed 2 minutes to develop a light crust. Then stir.
  5. Add cherry tomatoes and stir 1 minute. Add spinach and stir until wilted, about 90 seconds.
  6. Remove from heat. Squeeze lemon juice over everything.
Nutrition per serving: Calories: ~210   Potassium: ~380mg   Sodium: ~290mg   Protein: ~20g

Servings: 1

Recipe 4: Soy Curl Chik-Style Strips

Ready in 20 minutes • 1 pan • The base protein for many recipes in this book

Ingredients

  • 40g dry Butler Soy Curls
  • 240ml Chik-Style Broth
  • 15ml light olive oil
  • 2g garlic powder
  • 2g smoked paprika
  • 1g black pepper

Instructions

  1. Pour Chik-Style Broth over dry Soy Curls in a bowl. Let soak 10 minutes until softened.
  2. Squeeze out excess liquid gently — damp but not dripping.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan on medium-high. Add Soy Curls and spread into a single layer.
  4. Add garlic powder, smoked paprika, and pepper. Stir to coat.
  5. Cook 5 to 7 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes, until golden and slightly crispy on the edges.
Nutrition per serving: Calories: ~120   Potassium: ~180mg   Sodium: ~280mg   Protein: ~11g

Servings: 1

  • 185g cooked jasmine rice
  • 15ml light olive oil
  • 15ml Bragg Liquid Aminos
  • 5ml sesame oil
  • 20g green onions
  • 3g sesame seeds
  • 1g black pepper

Instructions

  1. Warm rice in microwave 60 to 90 seconds. Place in a bowl.

Recipe 6: Ground Turkey and Zucchini Skillet

Ready in 20 minutes • 1 pan • High protein meat meal

Ingredients

  • 170g ground turkey, 93% lean
  • 200g zucchini
  • 80g yellow onion
  • 8g garlic
  • 4g cumin
  • 3g smoked paprika
  • 2g garlic powder
  • 1g black pepper
  • 1g fine sea salt
  • 15ml light olive oil
  • 15ml fresh lime juice to finish

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan on medium-high. Add onion and cook 3 minutes.

Recipe 9: Simple Baked Tofu

Ready in 35 minutes • Oven method • Hands-off cooking

Ingredients

  • 350g extra firm tofu
  • 15ml Bragg Liquid Aminos
  • 15ml light olive oil
  • 3g garlic powder
  • 3g smoked paprika
  • 2g onion powder
  • 1g black pepper
  • 1g fine sea salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees C (400 degrees F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cut pressed tofu into 2cm cubes. Pat very dry.
  3. In a bowl mix Bragg, olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Add tofu cubes and toss gently to coat. Spread in a single layer on the baking sheet.
  5. Bake 25 to 28 minutes, flipping once at 12 minutes, until golden and firm.
  6. Let cool 5 minutes before serving.
Nutrition per serving: Calories: ~175   Potassium: ~160mg   Sodium: ~280mg   Protein: ~18g

Servings: 1

Recipe 14: Simple Fried Rice with Egg and Frozen Vegetables

Ready in 15 minutes • 1 pan • Use leftover rice

Ingredients

  • 278g cold cooked jasmine rice
  • 2 large eggs
  • 120g frozen peas and carrots mix
  • 40g green onions
  • 8g garlic
  • 30ml Bragg Liquid Aminos
  • 5ml sesame oil
  • 15ml light olive oil
  • 1g black pepper
  • 3g sesame seeds to garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan on high heat until very hot.

Recipe 19: Spanish Rice

Ready in 25 minutes • Stovetop • Great side dish or bowl base all week

Ingredients

  • 280g dry jasmine or long grain white rice
  • 480ml Pacific Foods Low Sodium Vegetable Broth
  • 200g WinCo diced tomatoes with liquid
  • 80g yellow onion
  • 8g garlic
  • 4g cumin
  • 3g smoked paprika
  • 2g garlic powder
  • 2g dried oregano
  • 1g fine sea salt
  • 1g black pepper
  • 15ml light olive oil
  • 15ml fresh lime juice to finish
  • 10g fresh cilantro to garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a medium saucepan on medium-high. Add onion and cook 4 minutes until softened.
  2. Add garlic, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper. Stir 30 seconds.
  3. Add dry rice and stir to coat every grain in the spiced oil. Toast the rice 2 minutes, stirring frequently, until grains look opaque and smell faintly nutty.
  4. Add broth and diced tomatoes with their liquid. Stir once. Bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce heat to the lowest setting. Cover tightly. Cook 18 minutes without lifting the lid.
  6. Remove from heat. Leave covered and undisturbed 5 minutes. Lift lid, fluff with a fork. Squeeze lime juice over the rice and fold in cilantro.
Nutrition per serving: Calories: ~280   Potassium: ~320mg   Sodium: ~390mg   Protein: ~6g

Servings: 1

Recipe 20: Chipotle-Style Cilantro Lime Rice

Ready in 20 minutes • Rice cooker • Restaurant-style with almost no effort

Ingredients

  • 280g dry jasmine rice
  • 420ml water
  • 3g fine sea salt
  • 30ml fresh lime juice — added AFTER rice is cooked
  • 15g fresh cilantro
  • 15ml extra virgin olive oil — added AFTER cooking
  • Zest of 1 lime — optional, adds extra lime brightness

Instructions

  1. Rinse dry rice in a fine mesh strainer under cold water for 30 seconds until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Add rinsed rice, water, and salt to your rice cooker. Press cook. Walk away — the rice cooker handles everything.
  3. When the rice cooker finishes and switches to warm, let it sit 5 minutes with the lid on before opening.
  4. Open the lid and fluff rice gently with a fork or rice paddle.
  5. Pour lime juice over the hot rice. Add extra virgin olive oil and optional lime zest. Fold everything together gently — do not stir aggressively or the rice becomes sticky.
  6. Add chopped cilantro and fold in gently. Taste and add a tiny pinch more salt if needed. Serve immediately.
Nutrition per serving: Calories: ~260   Potassium: ~55mg   Sodium: ~220mg   Protein: ~5g

Servings: 1
